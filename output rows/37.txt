To convert from degrees Fahrenheit to Celsius, we use the formula:

Celsius = (Fahrenheit - 32) * 5/9

Where:
* F is the temperature in degrees Fahrenheit
* C is the temperature in Celsius

So, 100 degrees Fahrenheit is equal to:
100°F - 32 * 5/9 = 100°F - 160/9 = 100°F - 18.88...

Therefore, 100 degrees Fahrenheit is equal to 100°C.
