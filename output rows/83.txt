Here are three examples of pull-ups:

1.  **The Barbell Pull-Up:** This is a classic and effective exercise that involves using a barbell at the bottom of the squat position, with your feet shoulder-width apart and your back straight. It's great for building strength and endurance.

2.  **The Inverted Rows:** This exercise involves pulling yourself up towards your chest while keeping your back straight. The muscles in your back are engaged, and you're essentially working on squeezing the pull through your abdominal muscles.

3.  **The Cable Pull-Up:** This is a more advanced exercise that uses a cable machine to lift your body up and down. It requires a good range of motion and can be challenging for beginners but offers significant muscle growth and strength gains.
