```sql
SELECT COUNT(*) AS user_count
FROM users;
```

**Explanation:**

* **`SELECT COUNT(*)`**: This is the core of the query. `COUNT(*)` counts all rows in the `users` table, regardless of whether they are null or not.  This effectively gives you the total number of records for each country.
* **`AS user_count`**: This assigns an alias to the calculated count column. This makes the output more readable and easier to understand.

**Alternative (using `GROUP BY` and `COUNT()`):**

You can also achieve this using a `GROUP BY` clause and `COUNT()` function:

